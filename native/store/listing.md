**Full description** (max 4,000)
```
kiln is a calm to-do app that understands how you talk.

Type "pay rent friday 5pm !high #home" and kiln files it for Friday at 5 PM, marks it high priority and puts it in Home. No forms, no menus. It reads Hinglish and Hindi too: "mummy ko kal 9 baje raat call" becomes a task for tomorrow at 9 PM.

TYPE IT HOW YOU SAY IT
• Dates: today, tomorrow, friday, next week, 26 oct, the 30th, kal, parso, agle hafte, shukravar
• Times: 5pm, 7:30 am, noon, 6 baje, subah 7 baje, raat 9 baje, saade 6 baje
• Priority with !high, !med or !low, and folders with #work, #home or any name
• You see what kiln understood before you save, so nothing surprises you

STAY ON TOP OF YOUR DAY
• Today, Upcoming and a calendar of everything due
• Reminders at the time you set
• Repeating tasks: daily, weekly, monthly
• Focus timer to work on one task at a time
• Habits with streaks, and a week of progress at a glance
• Ambient mode: a calm always-on screen with a clock, your day and Ember, a little flame that cheers you on

YOUR LANGUAGE
Use kiln in English, हिन्दी, मराठी, ગુજરાતી, বাংলা, தமிழ், తెలుగు, ಕನ್ನಡ, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ, മലയാളം, ଓଡ଼ିଆ or اردو.

PRIVATE AND SIMPLE
• Start without an account. Your tasks stay on your phone.
• Make a free account to sync with the web app at kiln.satpurastudio.com
• Works offline and syncs when you're back
• Back up your tasks to a file whenever you like
• Free, with no ads and no tracking

Made in Khandwa by Satpura Studio.
```
