Questions.

Do I need an account?

No. Choose “continue on this device” and everything stays in your browser. Make a free account when you want your tasks on more than one device.

Is there an app?

kiln runs in your browser on any phone or computer. On a phone, use “Add to Home Screen” and it opens full-screen like an app, works offline and can send reminders.

Where is my data stored?

Guest data stays on your device. With an account, it's stored securely with Supabase, and only you can read it. See the privacy policy.

Can I take my data out?

Yes. Settings → Export downloads everything as a file, and you can delete your account and all its data from Settings at any time.
