Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

kiln (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a task and habit manager built by Satpura Studio. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • If you create an account: your email address and the display name you choose.
  • Your content: the tasks, folders, habits, notes, dates and settings you create in the app.
  • Basic technical data needed to run the service (e.g. timestamps of when tasks are completed, used to show your own stats).

You can use kiln without an account (“continue on this device”) — in that mode your data stays only in your browser/device and never reaches our servers.

How we use it

  • To provide the app and sync your data across your devices when you’re signed in.
  • To send account emails (sign-up verification and password resets).
  • To show you your own progress and insights inside the app.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for advertising or tracking.

Where your data lives

When you sign in, your account and content are stored using these processors:

  • Supabase — authentication and database (data hosting).
  • Brevo — sending account verification and password-reset emails.
  • GitHub Pages / our host — serving the app itself.

Your choices & rights

  • Export: download all your data as a JSON file anytime (Settings → Export my data).
  • Delete: permanently delete your account and all synced data anytime (Settings → Danger zone → Delete account). This removes your auth record and your data from our database.
  • Access / correction: your data is fully editable inside the app.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, it is removed from our systems. Local (guest) data is removed when you clear it on your device.

Children

kiln is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date at the top. Significant changes will be noted in the app.
