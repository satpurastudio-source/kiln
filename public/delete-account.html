How to remove your kiln account and data

You can delete your kiln account at any time. Deleting it permanently removes your account (email and display name) and everything synced to it: tasks, folders, habits, notes and settings. It can’t be undone.

In the app (quickest)

  1. Open kiln and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Danger zone → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and synced data are deleted straight away.

Want a copy first? Use Settings → Download a backup before deleting.

Without the app

Email satpurastudio@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my kiln account”. We’ll delete the account and its data within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted, and what isn’t

  • Deleted: your account, email, display name and all synced content.
  • Not linked to you, so not affected: the anonymous usage counts described in the privacy policy. They carry no account, name or email.
  • Guest mode: if you never made an account, nothing is on our servers. Your data lives on your device; uninstall the app or clear the site’s data to remove it.
